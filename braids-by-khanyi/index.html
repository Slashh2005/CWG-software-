Know Your Length

Length Guide

Not sure what to call the length you want? Use this guide when booking — or ask us to help you choose.

Shoulder Length

Just slightly lower than the shoulders.

Bra Length

Must be slightly beneath the top part of the bra.

Waist Length

Must just graze the waist.

Bum Length

Must sit in the middle of the bum.

Custom Length

Extra-long / custom lengths available upon request.

  • Prices are starting ranges and may vary depending on braid size, length, hair density, colour, style, complexity and time required.
